## Competitive landscape

### How competitors make money

**Streaming (scale + subscriptions):**
- **Spotify**: €19.1B/yr run rate (Q2 2026), 300M premium subs, ARPU €4.89/mo. 89% subscription / 11% ads. Artist payout: ~$0.003–0.005/stream.
- **Tidal**: ~$70M/yr. Direct Artist Payouts model. Single tier $10.99/mo. Part of Block.

**Direct-to-consumer (commission on sales):**
- **Bandcamp**: $218M paid to artists (2025). 15% on digital, 10% on physical. Visibly wounded — 50% staff cut (2023), further layoffs June 2026.
- **EVEN**: 500K+ artists, UMG deal, avg $20+/release, ~20% platform fee. Sell-before-streaming model.
- **Weverse (HYBE)**: 25.2M digital products sold (2025), growing 30%/yr. 11–12M MAU. Proves digital goods sell at scale when framed as fandom.

### The graveyard (what not to be)

| Platform | What | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Royal.io | Fractional royalty NFTs | Shut down 2024 |
| OneOf | $63M "green" music NFTs | Collapsed |
| Sound.xyz | Music NFT drops | Offline Dec 2025 |
| Nina Protocol | Non-speculative, 100% artist | Shutdown May 2026 |

Root cause: *speculators, not fans.*

### Market sizing

- **Global recorded music**: $31.7B (2025), +6.4% YoY
- **Physical grew 8.0%** — vinyl at $1.04B in the US alone
- **Superfan monetization**: $4.3B/yr opportunity (Goldman Sachs)
- **US superfans**: 15% of population, spend $39/mo on physical, 73% buy merch (Luminate)
- **Afropop diaspora**: 121% higher music spend than avg US listener
- **Kenya streaming market**: ~$27M (2027) — too small for streaming, right for D2C
- **Bandcamp D2C**: $218M/yr proves fans pay $15–40 direct from artist

### Noizes positioning

- 85/15 split (beats EVEN's ~80/20, matches Bandcamp digital)
- File survives platform death (unlike Bandcamp)
- No crypto/NFT positioning (unlike the graveyard)
- Zero manufacturing cost (unlike vinyl variants)
- Artist brings their own audience (unlike marketplace-first approaches)

## Upside excluded from base model

The 200-drop / KES 3.4M stretch model deliberately excludes:
- Repeat drops from winning artists (+KES 750K–1.5M estimated)
- International USD sales at higher price points
- Resale transactions (15% commission, zero acquisition cost)
- Cross-artist collector purchases
- Distribution partnerships
- Breakout artists selling hundreds/thousands

KES 3.4M is the stretch operating target, not the ceiling.
