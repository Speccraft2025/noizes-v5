# Camera language

| Shot | Purpose | Duration | Movement | Fallback |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| Threshold Shot | Establish distance and ceremony | 12–18s | Almost still, 2% approach | Static wide |
| Breathing Frame | Let the house acknowledge the listener | 8–12s | Sub-perceptual scale pulse | Light pulse |
| Memory Push | Give recovered detail importance | 6–9s | Slow forward move with focus shift | Detail brightens |
| Recognition Close-Up | Make the key physically present | 5–7s | Restrained lateral parallax | Stable crop |
| Photograph Entry | Turn memory into space | 10–14s | Aperture-like push through frame | Cross-dissolve through paper texture |

Stillness is the default. Camera energy never exceeds the musical hierarchy.
