# Creative direction

## Thesis

Music does not accompany the house. Music is the force by which the house remembers its own construction.

## Vertical slice

**The Threshold — 00:00–01:24.** Curiosity becomes recognition. A sealed archival miniature is felt before it is seen. The recording reveals its edges, gives the architecture breath, draws a lyric into condensation, returns a brass key, and turns a photograph into a doorway.

## Archival dream realism

The image combines miniature stage architecture, charcoal plans, oxidized brass, imperfect glass, paper fiber, dust and restrained impossible geometry. Black is treated as material, not empty space. Warmth arrives only through recovered detail.

## Creative north star

`MUSIC AWAKENS MEMORY → MEMORY RECONSTRUCTS SPACE → SPACE REVEALS HISTORY → HISTORY BECOMES AN OBJECT`

The slice covers Dormant and Remembering. Overwhelmed and Preserved are designed for later gates but are not implemented now.

## Mood references

- A museum drawer opened in a dark conservation room.
- A paper theatre illuminated by one practical lamp.
- Condensation on old window glass.
- A graphite floor plan refusing to remain flat.
- A family photograph whose interior has measurable depth.

These are material references, not layout references. No external studio work or proprietary asset is reproduced.
