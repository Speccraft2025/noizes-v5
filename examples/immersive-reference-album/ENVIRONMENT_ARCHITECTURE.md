# Environment architecture

The Three.js world contains a conservation-space object, exterior terrain, full façade, pitched roof, side walls, porch deck, roof, columns, four steps, hinged door, two deep windows, twelve-metre interior hallway, floor, walls, ceiling, table, curtains, photograph frame, key, tree and atmospheric volumes. The hallway intentionally extends farther than the exterior permits.

The environment remains one scene. The opening case unfolds into the camera path; the world activates behind it. The photograph occupies the façade and masks a real continuation of the hall. There are no screen-to-screen scene changes.
