## Render review completed

The build was reviewed in the in-app Chromium browser against the locally served, fully offline package output. The review caught and corrected four faults that static validation could not see: incomplete Three.js namespace conversion, a debug-state serialization loop, timestamp previews that depended on media seek readiness, and a portrait camera that cropped the architecture too tightly.

The approved capture set is in `screenshots/full-world-review/`. It includes the object, fall, breathing house, inscription, physical key, photograph portal, passage alignment and a 390 × 844 portrait composition. The capture page at `screenshots/full-world-review/index.html` is deliberately development-only and is not added to the `.nz` package.

At the final desktop breathing frame the runtime reported 51 draw calls, 2,244 triangles, nine loaded textures and five lights in Cinematic mode. The key is visibly grounded on the console table through the opened doorway, and the photograph remains a physical plane until its transition beat.
