# Implementation summary and known limitations

Delivered: complete source, repeatable acquisition/build pipeline, five-track package, four synchronized lyric files, continuous-world experience, authored cue timelines, Guide/exploration, edition and simulated provenance, component hashes, rights ledger, validator, tests, reports, and template specifications.

Known limitations: source recordings are legally safest in the U.S.; other territories require local term analysis. Historical acoustic recordings do not permit true gapless joins, so transitions are visual rather than sample-accurate crossfades. LRC synchronization was manually estimated and should receive a final ear-led editorial pass. The reference renderer uses art-directed 2D perspective rather than bundled Three.js, prioritizing portability and fallback behavior. Development signatures are placeholders, never claims of platform authentication.
