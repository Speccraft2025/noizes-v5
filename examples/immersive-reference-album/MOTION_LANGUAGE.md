# Motion language

- Architecture moves with mass: long anticipation, low-frequency breath, no idle drift.
- Memory gathers through opacity, texture and depth; it never pops in.
- Paper, dust and condensation can react quickly, then settle.
- The camera remains still until movement has narrative meaning.
- Major actions receive recovery time and are never stacked.
- Authored movement is reconciled to audio time; ambient dust may continue softly while paused.
- Pointer presence displaces nearby dust and changes parallax by no more than 1.5 degrees.
- Reduced motion replaces travel with light, focus and material revelation.
