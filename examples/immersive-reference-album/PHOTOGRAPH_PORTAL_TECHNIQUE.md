# Photograph portal technique

The generated fictional hall image begins as a mapped paper plane inside a deep wooden frame. Chemical development is represented by increasing contrast and opacity. A second plane behind it becomes a dark spatial mask while the real hallway aligns with the photograph’s perspective. The hinged exterior door opens as the camera approaches. The frame remains visible until the camera crosses its depth, after which fog density and spatial-audio filtering change together.

This is geometry continuity, not a full-screen image zoom.
