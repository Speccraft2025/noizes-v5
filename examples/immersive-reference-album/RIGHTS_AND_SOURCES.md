# Rights and sources

Verified 2026-08-01. All five source recordings were published before 1923 and are identified by the Library of Congress National Jukebox rights statement as having entered the U.S. public domain on 2022-01-01. Source-page HTML, original MP3s, direct-download URLs, conversions, and SHA-256 hashes are preserved locally. International terms may differ; this demo makes a U.S. public-domain determination and records that limitation.
