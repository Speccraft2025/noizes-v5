# Immersive scene schema 0.1

`house.scene.json` declares a continuous scene identity, art direction, ordered rooms, track bindings, and fallback. Objects have a stable ID, one of `NARRATIVE`, `MUSIC`, `ARCHIVAL`, `VISUAL`, `CREDITS`, `RIGHTS`, `PROVENANCE`, or `TECHNICAL`, plus an explicit `fictional` flag. Narrative objects may never be presented as archival facts.
