# Track experience treatment

## 1 — The Threshold

Curiosity → recognition. Front hall; doorframe motif; cold moonlight becoming oxidized amber; paper, glass and aged wood. Threshold Shot, Memory Push and Photograph Entry. The returned key is the primary object. The door breathes, the lyric appears in condensation, and a photograph becomes the first interior doorway. Accumulation: the threshold, window and key remain in the house.

## 2 — The Parlour

Warmth → fragility. Parlour; recurring window; tobacco, velvet and faded silver; late-afternoon amber. Witness Shot and Object Orbit. Photographs first show empty rooms, then acquire traces during vocal phrases. The curtain becomes a projected memory surface. Transition: a circular portrait mount becomes a kitchen clock. Accumulation: photographs remain embedded in later walls.

## 3 — The Kitchen

Life → absence. Kitchen; clock and table; enamel, steam and worn linen; warm practical light draining to blue-grey. Recognition Close-Up and Breathing Frame. Recipe cards and absent shadows repeat domestic actions incorrectly. Transition: the clock face becomes a child’s paper moon. Accumulation: the extended table cuts through the later staircase.

## 4 — The Child’s Room / Staircase

Wonder → disorientation. Bedroom and impossible stair; doorframe and wallpaper; chalk, paper and painted wood; moonlit cobalt. Impossible Dolly and Spatial Match Cut. Drawings detach into architecture; scale changes from adult to child. Transition: a drawn tree grows through the roof and becomes the dawn garden. Accumulation: earlier rooms appear behind impossible doors.

## 5 — Archive and Garden at Dawn

Grief → acceptance → preservation. Overlapping house and garden; every recurring motif; translucent paper, archival board and morning glass. Loss Pullback, Archive Overhead and Final Preservation Shot. Memories expose the package’s historical and technical layers. The house resolves into a miniature inside the `.nz` object. Final change: the whole organism becomes inspectable without ceasing to be emotional.

Only Track One’s first 84 seconds is implemented at Gate 2.
