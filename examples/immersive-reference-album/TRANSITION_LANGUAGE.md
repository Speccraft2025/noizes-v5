# Transition language

The recurring transition vocabulary is Photograph Entry, Architectural Fold and Dust Reconstruction. Gate 2 implements Photograph Entry: the returned key rotates, its bow becomes the photograph aperture, silver emulsion fills the frame, and the exterior doorway resolves as an interior wall. The next room is implied, not yet built.

Transitions occur in world space. Black frames may be used as physical occlusion, never as page-loading screens.
