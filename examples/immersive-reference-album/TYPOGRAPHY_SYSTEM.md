# Typography system

- **Whisper:** small peripheral narrative, brief and optional.
- **Inscription:** object- or architecture-bound labels.
- **Environmental lyric:** stable words revealed through condensation, paper or shadow.
- **Declaration:** rare, full-frame language at structural moments.
- **Archive:** stable semantic reading mode.

Gate 2 uses an environmental lyric on the door glass and an inscription on the returned key. Typography is segmented by words, masked by lines and kept readable. Stable lyric text is always available through the accessibility drawer.
